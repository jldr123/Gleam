Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Gleam doesn't collect your reflections or anything else about you. Everything you write stays on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Gleam saves these on your device only, and never sends them to us or anyone else:

  • Your reflections: moods, gratitudes, answers, notes, letters and starred moments.
  • Your settings: first name (if you add one), theme, rhythm, reflection time and lighter nights.
  • Small bits of app state, such as when you last saved a backup and your answers to the occasional "Is Gleam helping?" question.
  • When you send a thank-you, the name and the time you sent it. The message itself goes straight from you to them.

You can delete all of it at any time with Settings, then Erase all data. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes it too.

Backups and exports

When you save a backup or a readable copy, Gleam creates the file on your phone, and you choose where it goes, for example Files or iCloud Drive. Gleam never receives a copy. Restoring reads the file on your phone.

Reminders

The calendar reminder is a file created on your phone that you add to your own calendar. In the App Store version, evening notifications are scheduled on your phone by the operating system. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Services Gleam relies on

  • Hosting. The Gleam website is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel receives standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, when the page loads. Your reflections are never part of this.
  • Fonts. The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives the same standard request information.
  • Support link. "If things feel heavy" opens findahelpline.com, an independent service with its own privacy policy.
  • App stores. If you download Gleam from the App Store, Apple handles the download and any purchase under its own privacy policy.

Children

Gleam is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Gleam will never start collecting your reflections.
